Overview
This is a Durham County court resource for small claims and summary ejectment filing logistics. It is most useful for local contact information, filing location, filing fees, and forms.

Summary ejectment filing help
The page provides a local summary ejectment packet for self-represented filers.
For summary ejectment, Durham County lists these commonly required forms:
- Complaint in Summary Ejectment, AOC-CVM-201
- Magistrate Summons, AOC-CVM-100
- Servicemembers Civil Relief Act Declaration, AOC-G-250

Durham County also lists common filing requirements:
- a $96 filing fee payable to the Durham County Clerk of Court
- a $30 service fee per defendant payable to the Durham County Sheriff's Department
- a stamped envelope addressed to each defendant
- if filing by mail, a self-addressed stamped envelope for return copies

The page says parties may call ahead to schedule a court date for summary ejectment, and that scheduling ahead is mandatory if filing five or more cases.

Important limitation
This county page is mainly about filing procedure and local logistics.
It is not a general explanation of tenant defenses or housing rights, and court staff cannot give legal advice.

Key takeaways
- Useful for Durham-specific filing logistics.
- Good source for clerk contact information, mailing address, local fee amounts, and form names.
- Best paired with broader statewide landlord-tenant guidance for substantive rights and defenses.
